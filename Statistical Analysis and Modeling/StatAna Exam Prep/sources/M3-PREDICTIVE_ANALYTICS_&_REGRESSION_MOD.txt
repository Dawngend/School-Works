independent variables.

Coefficient of Multiple Determination(R2)
The
coefficient
of
multiple
determination,
R2,
is
a
goodness-of-fit measure
R2 is a figure of merit; the higher the R2, the better is the
success of the model in explaining the variation in the
response using the set of predictors.

Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)
The ANOVA is a decomposition of the total variation in the
response into explained (pattern) and unexplained (error)
parts.
The explained variability is the amount of variation in the
response variable that may be attributed to the predictors
explicitly state in the model.
The unexplained variability is the amount of variation
attributed to random error.

t-tests
The t-test helps in assessing if an individual predictor is significant
Variable 1:TV Ad
• Since p<0.05, TV ad spend is a significant predictor of sales.
Variable 2: Radio Ad
• Since p<0.05, radio ad spend is a significant predictor of sales.
Variable 3 : Newspaper Ad
Since p > 0.05 newspaper ad spend is an insignificant predictor of sales.
